## The Greek Alphabet with Handwriting Baseline

![](/assets/images/das-griechische-alphabet.png)

The image shows the Greek letter β, both the uppercase and the lowercase form. The letter is placed between handwriting guidelines (baseline, x-height line, etc.), as commonly used in primary school.

The following [PDF](/physik-lehrmittel/PDFs/Greek-Alphabet.pdf) contains the entire alphabet with these guideline lines.
