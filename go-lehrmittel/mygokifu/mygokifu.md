# Go Teaching Materials

## Go Kifu

### My Go Kifu (English)

For download:

- as an [A4 sheet](../PDFs/mygokifu-A4.pdf) for printing individual pages
- in [A5 format](../PDFs/mygokifu-A5.pdf) for printing as a booklet. In copy shops, this can usually be produced at low cost.
- in [A3 format](../PDFs/mygokifu-A3.pdf) for independently producing A5 booklets: Print the PDF on A3 paper, double-sided, binding on the short edge. Then cut lengthwise, staple from the outside, fold, done. You will need an extra-long stapler, however.
