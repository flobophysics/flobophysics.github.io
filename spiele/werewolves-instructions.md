# Werewolves, Game Instructions

### What is “Werewolves”?

“Werewolves” is an exciting roleplaying game. All players belong to a village – but dangerous werewolves are hiding among them! Every night they strike in secret. During the day, the village tries to expose the werewolves and drive them away. Who will win in the end – the village or the werewolves?

Suitable for: 8-20 people,\
Age: from approx. 13 years,\
Duration: 20-30 minutes

### Basic Game Principle

- All players are secretly given a role.
- There are two teams:
  - **Werewolves** - they want to eat all the villagers.
  - **Villagers** - they want to discover and chase away the werewolves.
- Each round consists of a **night** and a **day**.
- During the night, the werewolves attack. During the day, the players discuss and vote on who will be executed (eliminated from the game).
- The game ends when either all the werewolves are dead or the werewolves have eaten all the villagers.

## Game Phases

The day and night phases alternate until the game ends.

### Night Phase

- Everyone closes their eyes.
- The moderator calls out roles one after the other, e.g:
  - Seer (looks at a role card)
  - Werewolves (choose a victim)
  - Witch (can heal or poison)
- The night ends when all special roles have been active.

### Day Phase

- The moderator announces who died during the night.
- There is a discussion about who could be a werewolf.
- The village then votes on who should be executed.
- The executed person is eliminated – their role is revealed by turning over the card.

### End of the Game

The game ends when:

- all werewolves are dead, i.e. victory for the village
- the werewolves have eaten all the villagers, i.e. victory for the werewolves
- the two lovers are the last ones left, i.e. joint victory for the couple.

## Instructions for the Moderator

The moderator is the most important role of the whole game. They first hand out the cards with the roles and, if necessary, answer questions [about the roles](werewolves-roles).

Order on the first night:

1.  Everyone goes to sleep,
2.  the thief swaps his card, the stolen person is tapped,
3.  Cupid decides on the lovers,
4.  the lovers show each other their cards,
5.  Seer looks at someone’s card,
6.  Werewolves choose a victim while the girl peeps,
7.  Witch is told the victim and decides on two potions,
8.  the whole village wakes up.

Order on the following nights:

- Seer
- Werewolves
- Witch
- village

During the day, the eliminated players are named first and must reveal their cards.

### The Accusations

The discussions and accusations are the phase of the game with the most fun. The village talks about the murder; you try to tell from the others whether they are lying and whether they are actually a werewolf.

During the villagers’ discussion, accusations can be made and the accused stand up. Each defendant is allowed to defend themselves once, after which a vote is taken. Whoever has the most votes is eliminated.
If someone is eliminated, he or she may no longer speak or give any clues.

### The Role of the Moderator

The moderator has the most important role of the game.
The fun of the whole group depends on him. It goes without saying that the moderator must be impartial and not give the group any hints.

If the number of players is small, you can get by **without a moderator**. A temporary moderator distributes the cards as usual, including one for himself. He coordinates the first night, in which only the werewolves awaken: they recognise each other, but do not kill any villagers. Whoever is eliminated from the village in the first round then takes on the role of moderator.
