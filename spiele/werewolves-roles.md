## Roles in the Game ‘Werewolves’

The roles of the players are assigned by drawing a card at the beginning. It is generally forbidden to reveal your role. The card is only revealed when a player leaves the game.

### Werewolves

- They know each other because they wake up together.
- They choose a victim together every night. Their aim is to eliminate all the villagers.
- Otherwise they pretend to be on the village team.

### Villagers

- Have no special ability.
- Vote in the execution.
- Their goal is to discover and get rid of the werewolves.

### Seer

- May check one person’s card each night.
- The gamemaster then secretly shows her the role of this person.
- May only use her knowledge carefully in the discussion.

### The Witch

has two potions:

- A **healing potion** to save a person who has been killed – including herself. The symbol for the game master is the thumbs up.
- Some **poison** to kill another person. The symbol is the thumbs down.

Each potion may only be used once in the game. Once the potions have been used up, the witch no longer wakes up.

### Hunter

If the hunter dies, he may shoot another person. Both are then eliminated at the same time. The hunter may not justify or comment on his decision.

## Roles for advanced players

### Cupid

only together with the lovers:

- is only active on the first night,
- induces two people as lovers,
- otherwise a simple villager.

### The Lovers

only together with Cupid:

- awaken once after Cupid to show their cards to each other.
- When one lover dies, the other dies of grief; both die together.
- If the lovers are from different teams, they only win together as the last two survivors.

### Thief

- On the first night, the thief may swap cards with another player.
- Afterwards, the stolen card gives him a new role.
- The player who was robbed of their original role is tapped by the gamemaster: he now knows that he is a simple villager.

### Siblings

The siblings awake once in the first night. They realise who the other sibling is and know they are on the same team.

### The Little Girl

The girl doesn‘t wake up at night. But when it’s the werewolves’ turn, she can blink, i.e. secretly peek to find out who the werewolves are. She must be careful in order not to become the werewolves’ prey imminently.
The little girl is not allowed to make the werewolves think she is a werewolf too.

This is a very powerful role that makes sense for larger groups playing *Werewolves*.

### Angel

The Angel wakes up before the Werewolves’ turn at night. He points at a fellow player, thereby protecting him or her: if the Werewolves attack that player, they will not be killed. The Angel cannot protect himself.

### Village Tramp

- The village tramp wakes up at night before the werewolves. He tells the game master where he wants to spend the night.
- If the village tramp is to be killed during the night, he survives.
- However, if the player with whom the village mattress spends the night is killed, both of them are eliminated from the game.
